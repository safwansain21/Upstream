## Important resolved decisions

- The chosen style is the **orange/ice-blue hybrid with flowing edges**.
- Use animation to communicate navigation and evidence changes, not decorate every component.
- Reports may start without a mapped or named river.
- Scientific localization requires verified prerequisites; an unmapped report is still a useful case.
- No water safety diagnosis, sewage classifier, invented confidence percentages or clinical patient fiction.
- The exact engine implementation in the scientific companion supersedes earlier conversational proposals.
- Field usefulness remains a validation question; build the validation machinery without faking the result.
- External accounts/secrets are deployment configuration, not permission to simulate successful integrations.
